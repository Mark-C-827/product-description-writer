# Category Playbooks

What shoppers decide on, per category. Route here after identifying the
category in step 1.

Each entry: the decision axis, specs that matter, objections to pre-answer, and
the failure mode to avoid.

---

## Apparel & footwear

**Decides on:** fit, fabric feel, care, how it looks on a real body.

**Lead with:** the fit/fabric truth - that is what they are actually buying.
**Specs that earn their place:** fabric composition and weight (GSM), stretch,
lining, closure, inseam/rise, model measurements **and** garment measurements.
**Pre-answer:** runs small/large, shrinkage, sheerness, color accuracy, care.
**Details block:** size chart reference, fabric %, care symbols in words, origin.
**Avoid:** "flattering" without saying why; fabric romance with no numbers.

---

## Consumer electronics

**Decides on:** does it do the thing, with what I own, for how long.

**Lead with:** capability or the pain removed - not the chipset.
**Specs that earn their place:** battery (with conditions), ports/protocols,
compatibility list, dimensions/weight, warranty, in-the-box.
**Pre-answer:** compatibility, battery reality, app/subscription dependency,
updates, warranty.
**Details block:** full spec table, box contents, warranty terms.
**Avoid:** spec dumps above the fold; "blazing fast"; jargon the buyer does not
use.

---

## Beauty & skincare

**Decides on:** will it work on my skin, what does it feel like, is it safe.

**Lead with:** the sensory/result experience the buyer wants - carefully,
without outcome claims.
**Specs that earn their place:** key actives with %, texture/finish, scent,
volume, patch-test guidance, what it excludes.
**Pre-answer:** irritation, timeline, suitability for skin type, layering.
**Details block:** full INCI if available, volume, usage, storage.
**Avoid:** medical outcome claims, "clinically proven" without the study,
miracle language.

---

## Home & furniture

**Decides on:** does it fit my space, survive my life, arrive intact.

**Lead with:** the space or usage problem solved.
**Specs that earn their place:** dimensions (all three), weight capacity,
materials and finish, assembly, delivery type.
**Pre-answer:** fit, assembly, delivery access, care/cleaning.
**Details block:** full dimensions, materials, care, what's included.
**Avoid:** dimension-free romance; "easy assembly" without saying how easy.

---

## Food & beverage

**Decides on:** taste, occasion, what is in it.

**Lead with:** sensory - the actual flavor or moment.
**Specs that earn their place:** ingredients, allergens, origin, servings,
shelf life, storage, format/weight.
**Pre-answer:** allergens, shelf life, servings, storage after opening.
**Details block:** ingredients, allergens in bold, nutrition if labeled, net weight.
**Avoid:** health claims; sourcing claims without verification.

---

## Tools & hardware

**Decides on:** tough enough, right for my task, serviceable.

**Lead with:** the job it does that the alternative botches.
**Specs that earn their place:** material and hardness, ratings/load limits,
dimensions, weight, compatibility, warranty.
**Pre-answer:** durability for the use case, fitment, serviceability, warranty.
**Details block:** materials, ratings, dimensions, warranty.
**Avoid:** lifestyle framing; emotion; imprecise capability.

---

## Outdoor & gear

**Decides on:** performance in conditions, weight, packability.

**Lead with:** the condition it handles.
**Specs that earn their place:** waterproof/insulation ratings (with the
standard), weight, packed size, temperature rating, materials, warranty.
**Pre-answer:** real-world rating vs. lab rating, weight, repairability.
**Details block:** ratings with standards named, weight, materials, care.
**Avoid:** unverified ratings; "all-conditions" claims.

---

## Auto parts & accessories

**Decides on:** fits my vehicle, installs without drama, lasts.

**Lead with:** fitment or the problem solved.
**Specs that earn their place:** year/make/model coverage, material and finish,
installation type, ratings (IP/UV/salt), warranty.
**Pre-answer:** fitment, install difficulty, warranty implications, weather durability.
**Details block:** full fitment list, materials, ratings, install notes.
**Avoid:** universal "fits most" without the list; performance claims without data.

---

## Jewelry & accessories

**Decides on:** materials, craft, meaning, wearability.

**Lead with:** the material truth or the meaning - never "luxury."
**Specs that earn their place:** metal and purity, stone specs (carat, cut,
clarity, origin if known), dimensions, weight, closure, plating thickness.
**Pre-answer:** tarnish, sizing, plating longevity, care.
**Details block:** all material specs, dimensions, care.
**Avoid:** "luxury," "timeless," "investment piece" as filler; vague "gold tone."

---

## Baby & kids

**Decides on:** safety, age appropriateness, cleanability.

**Lead with:** the safety or ease truth.
**Specs that earn their place:** age/weight range, materials, standards met
(named), care, dimensions.
**Pre-answer:** safety standards, age range, cleaning, assembly.
**Details block:** age/weight range, materials, named standards, care.
**Avoid:** unverified safety claims; cuteness replacing information.

---

## Digital products & software

**Decides on:** what it does, what it works with, what it costs over time.

**Lead with:** the outcome in the user's workflow.
**Specs that earn their place:** platform support, integrations, limits
(seats/storage/usage), update cadence, support terms.
**Pre-answer:** platform compatibility, subscription vs. one-time, data export,
cancellation.
**Details block:** requirements, limits, terms.
**Avoid:** feature lists with no outcome; "AI-powered" as a benefit.

---

## Cross-category reminders

- **High-consideration purchases** (price, risk, research): more objections,
  longer Details block, proof over persuasion.
- **Low-consideration purchases**: hook + 3 bullets + specs. Do not over-write.
- **Giftable products**: add who it suits and the presentation; gifting risk is
  "will they like it."
- **Replenishable products**: mention the usage interval - it drives repeat purchase.
